# HR Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee demographics, income, attrition, and workforce distribution using Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into employee characteristics, attrition patterns, income levels, departments, and workforce distribution. It was developed as a portfolio project to practice HR data analysis, data cleaning, DAX, data visualization, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## Business Understanding

The purpose of this project was to analyze workforce characteristics and identify patterns related to employee attrition, income, demographics, and department distribution.

The project was selected to practice HR data analysis using Power BI and to demonstrate how employee data can be transformed into meaningful workforce insights.

One of the main challenges was preparing the employee dataset for analysis and creating measures to evaluate attrition, income, and workforce characteristics.

---

## Dataset

**IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance**

The dataset contains employee-level information covering demographics, job roles, departments, income, job satisfaction, years at the company, and employee attrition.

The dataset was selected because it provides a practical HR scenario for analyzing workforce characteristics and employee attrition using Power BI.

**Source:** [Kaggle – IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset)

**License:** Open Database

- Prepared the dataset for analysis in Power BI.

---

## Analytical Approach

The analysis followed these main steps:

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Reviewed the employee dataset.
   - Removed unnecessary and constant columns.
   - Standardized column names and data types.

2. **DAX Analysis**
   - Created measures to calculate total employees, attrition, attrition rate, average income, average age, and average years at the company.

3. **Data Visualization**
   - Created interactive dashboards using KPIs, charts, and slicers.
   - Visualized workforce distribution, attrition, income, demographics, and department performance.

4. **Business Insights**
   - Analyzed employee attrition patterns.
   - Compared workforce distribution across departments and demographic groups.
   - Examined attrition patterns across departments, job roles, and overtime status.

## Business Insights

- Research & Development has the largest workforce among the departments.
- Sales has the highest average monthly income among the departments.
- Laboratory Technician has the highest attrition count among the job roles.
- Employees who worked overtime recorded a higher number of attrition cases than employees who did not work overtime.
- The overall attrition rate was 16.12%, with 237 employees recorded as having left the company.
